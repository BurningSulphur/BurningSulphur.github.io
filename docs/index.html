Home Page

Welcome to my homepage. In the early days of the internet your home page was meant to be your own and not a generic search engine. Instead of search engines there would be lists of links you would navigate by. I keep a copy of this website in a folder on my desktop and set it as the homepage in my browser, then when I find something cool I add to it. You are welcome to copy this page to make it your own, but I recommend just making one yourself by making a txt file, renaming it homepage.html and starting here
